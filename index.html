🔬 Bilateral Retrodeformation

This tool removes taphonomic deformation from bilaterally symmetric structures (e.g. skulls) using paired landmarks placed on both sides of the specimen. It works on 3D meshes (OBJ/PLY) and on 2D images (PNG/JPG, e.g. a photograph in dorsal or ventral view), where the symmetry plane becomes a midline.

Algorithm (Schlager et al. 2018):

  • Symmetry plane — estimated from the mean of unit vectors connecting each right–left landmark pair.
  • Symmetrization — left landmarks are reflected across the plane, averaged with the right-side counterpart; the symmetric left position is then the mirror of the resulting right position.
  • TPS warp — a Thin Plate Spline deformation maps all mesh vertices from the asymmetric configuration to the symmetric one.

Equivalent to Morpho::retrodeform() in R and to bilat.symmetry() (object symmetry) in geomorph. The α parameter controls TPS regularization — increase it if the result shows unexpected warping near the landmarks.

Landmark placement: click Right side first (🔴), then the corresponding Left side (🔵), repeating for each pair. A minimum of 2 pairs is required; 5–10 well-distributed pairs typically give the best results.

Schlager S, Rüdell A, Wachter R (2018). Morpho and Rvcg — Shape Analysis in R. In: Statistical Shape and Deformation Analysis, pp. 217–256. Academic Press.
Adams DC, Collyer ML, Kaliontzopoulou A (2024). Geomorph: Software for geometric morphometric analyses. R package v4.0.
